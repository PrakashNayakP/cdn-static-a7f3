Belaku is a free Android app for making posters in Kannada. Festival greetings, invitations, shop notices, quotes for social media — the kind of thing that usually means fighting with fonts and English-only design tools. Belaku is built for Kannada from the ground up.

Get it on Google Play

What it does

  • Type in English, get Kannada. Write namaskara on an ordinary keyboard and it becomes ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ — no separate Kannada keyboard needed.
  • Ready-made templates for festivals, invitations, collages and traditional designs.
  • Kannada quotes and stickers, searchable the same way — type preeti and find quotes about ಪ್ರೀತಿ.
  • Over 30 Kannada fonts, with colour, shadow, outline and highlight controls.
  • Remove backgrounds from your photos in one tap.
  • Save at full resolution and share straight to WhatsApp or Instagram.

Privacy

No sign-up, and no account. The photos and posters you work with are processed on your device and are never uploaded to us. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.
